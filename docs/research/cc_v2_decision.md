Primary SoG-residual+combined selector was chosen on source-only validation/risk records before target errors. Its31.5% risk80 improvement over strong matched direct C+ is a positive component result. However, cheap GW+ridge has lower full mean and an inconclusive risk80 difference; exploratory one-seed GW residual also has a lower full mean. Hence the next model should not be chosen by cherry-picking this test table. Preserve the SoG result as measured, keep all controls, and select a future design with independent multi-camera source validation and a newly frozen test population.

No foundation model, target-camera CCM, cross-image test-camera calibration, paid compute or proprietary facial data was required. The current core operates on preprocessed linear input. Arbitrary smartphone ISP output, skin-specific color accuracy, cosmetics outcomes and patentability remain unvalidated. Strong compact published-method reproduction (e.g. FFCC after exact terms/protocol verification) is still missing; no SOTA claim is justified.
