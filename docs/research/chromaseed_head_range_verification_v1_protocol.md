# HR full verification and measurement protocol v1

The previous continuation is progress: six P2 auxiliary encoders were trained and independently verified. HR remains live; its final quality is unknown. This protocol governs new verification/measurement consumers without changing any of the 185 frozen training sources, the source lock, recovery adapter or model-selection rule.

## Full audit

The main audit must reject a live, failed or ambiguously alive primary. A completed job record is necessary but not sufficient: the recorded process must be confirmed dead. Caller must also obtain the primary tool session's actual terminal success. No full-audit result or final verification seal may be written while the primary is active. A separately labelled CPU-only probe may inspect already completed inner banks without claiming full completion.

Verify the HR source/input bindings, the sealed AS parent and every recovery binding. Inspect all126new inner banks: reconstruct the registered role and person-disjoint folds, original warm parents, native fit-only normalizers, token statistics, weighted sampler draws, schedule/rates, trace counts, model shapes and numeric bytes. Reconstruct new candidates with an independent metric reduction on the validated saved predictions.

Verify exact lineage and metadata remapping, and identify them as reused controls. Do not pretend they were newly fitted in HR. Reconstruct all384candidate scores and87choices, including per-pair/per-architecture/overall selections and stable original tie ordering. Compare all choices with the pre-final selections.json.

Inspect42new final banks and all252saved final-slot model payloads. Check the126selected model files against their chosen bank slot, and reproduce all50316selected final query examples through actual single-example NumPy calls, comparing every recurrent pass. Verify final native metrics, row indices, parameter counts, selected flags and207unique result identities. The inherited81records must exactly match the AS parent and frozen HR selection; no post-hoc winner substitution.

Bind all audit sources, contracts and inputs before full processing. Save dependencies, artifact hashes, counts and maximum drift. Existing completed receipts are preserved; only read-only verification may be repeated once sealed. The recovery adapter is separately bound and does not alter the numerical source lock.

## Runtime and construction measurements

Run only after actual primary success and full audit, with no competing GPU fit or CPU benchmark. Use the existing deterministic host/thread settings. Measure actual single-example CPU response for all189AS/HR architecture/head/seed combinations, with20warmups and3passes over64fixed evenly spaced rows each. Include constructor time and cached bytes; native photo decoding/segmentation and phone hardware are outside this measurement. NP/WE timings remain explicitly inherited and are not mixed into a fresh speed comparison.

For each of42new selected architecture/head/role banks, reconstruct all three original upstream NP parents and the complete six-slot continuation at the selected checkpoint. Require bitwise equality of every252continuation export and126selected predictions. Time the full recipe, not an extrapolation from per-step speed or division of a parallel bank by six. Reused unit construction costs may be cited from the sealed AS run with provenance. Report new measurements and inherited ones separately.
