# Universal compact photometric model — research plan

Latest steering broadens the focus from camera transfer to a lightweight, highly accurate model across scenes, surfaces, illumination and image processing. Camera is one nuisance factor. Universal here is an intended photometric operating domain, not arbitrary tasks or a measured guarantee. Preserve V3's failed architecture and all earlier milestones.

Use the active brainstorming/deep-research/subagent-driven workflow. Existing autonomous-work authorization resolves routine scope/format choices: a comprehensive source-cited Markdown research artifact, then concrete equations, ablations and local real-data experiments. No new approval, cloud resource, image upload or proprietary acquisition is required.

Run three independent bounded primary-source research tasks in parallel: (1) contemporary leading-lab visual self-supervision, teacher/student transfer and data curriculum; (2) compact/adaptive/iterative architectures and distillation with actual inference/rights constraints; (3) physics-grounded photometric supervision, intervention/equivariance and nearest prior art. Each writes a separate note, states dates/access limits, separates code/weight/data terms and does not download models/data or train. The root integrates these with the measured V1–V3 failures and designs a new mechanism; do not select the final idea before comparing alternatives.

The synthesis must distinguish borrowed training principles from the proposed technical contribution. Any learned target from real image transformations must state the transformation law and where it fails; controlled real-image augmentation is not an independent real benchmark. Do not manufacture surface DeltaE or assume restoration to an unknown original color is identifiable from every single image.

Rank candidate mechanisms by expected source accuracy, illumination/scene/surface/processing robustness, selective risk, compute/VRAM, training-data requirements, legitimate public supervision, overlap and time to a decisive experiment. Keep1–5M preferred/<10M desirable; RTX4060 8GB training; single-image local inference. Larger teachers may be studied or used in an explicitly affordable/cleared training path; no teacher/foundation/cloud dependency at deployed inference. Unknown commercial weights/data terms prohibit assuming production clearance.

Do useful primary-source research beyond color constancy, but reject fashionable techniques with no explicit causal connection to this task. Finish with an implementable architecture/training specification, strongest matched baseline, preserved negative controls, source-only selection and genuinely held-out evaluation. The previously planned global-color graph is only one candidate; the new steering authorizes replacing it if another mechanism is more defensible.
