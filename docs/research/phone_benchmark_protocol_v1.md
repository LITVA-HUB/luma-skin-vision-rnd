# Frozen phone transfer experiment v1

Predeclare before the first reserved Beyond RGB image/GT decoding. Reuse the
unchanged phone_reference_protocol_v1.md quality/preprocessing rules. Population:
44 complete official field TEST scenes, paired Samsung/Oppo,88 NT inputs.
Only the three distinct official TRAIN loader scenes have been inspected.

Primary comparison: frozen V2 SoG-residual MobileNetV3-large versus matched V2
direct MobileNetV3-large C+, both gain_aug0, seeds17/29/43. Each uses its already
source-fitted combined risk head, scale and calibration scores. Compare the
previously selected conventional gw_ridge1 and direct_hgb7 controls with their
frozen combined heads. Classical Gray World, Max RGB, Shades of Gray(p6) and
Gray Edge reuse the full masked-image implementation. Classical selective
scores are the mean recovery angle to the four classical estimates, including
self; this is a predeclared uncalibrated disagreement control, not fitted risk.

Secondary exploratory matrix: all six V5 arms, three seeds, their existing best
checkpoints, unchanged51-query two-stage readout. Point-only uses its base point
and has no trained risk head; other arms use raw angular critic risk. No new
threshold calibration for V5. Do not select a phone-winning seed/checkpoint or
present the best of18 as a confirmatory effect. Report every candidate/family.
Every estimator is source-only trained; no phone calibration or adaptation.

Immutable method lock binds all30 method definitions, model/head/calibration
files, exact executable Python, frozen data selection, acquisition verification
and both protocols. V2 numerical Python must match its original source snapshot;
only changed dependency declarations (h5py added for HDF5 preparation) may differ.
Do not weaken old V2 verifiers or overwrite old runs/external predictions. A new
runner independently verifies old checkpoint/config/head bindings and writes
new artifacts. No original training, selector or deployment source is modified.

Preparation verifies acquisition SHA for every consumed file before decoding.
Store all88 rows and exclusion reasons. Invalid polygon/HDF5/reference data is
unscorable, never zero-error. Keep NT inputs in an input-only cache, separate
from GT/reference files; prediction reads no WT/reference/GT file. Estimator
input is only128x128 RGB (classicals additionally use full resolution). Camera
and scene IDs are grouping metadata for reports, never neural features. Record
author privacy mask paths without modifying the supplied blur policy.

Report recovery and reproduction mean, median, trimean, best/worst25%, p90/p95,
catastrophic fractions>10°/>20°, and maxima, separately for Samsung, Oppo and
pooled inputs. Scorable population depends only on fixed reference integrity.
Mandatory model refusals cannot be accepted to reach nominal coverage. Report
fixed100/95/90/80/70/60% risk, full curve, achieved coverage among scorable and
all planned inputs, and actual source-calibrated threshold performance for
methods with frozen calibration. Reference exclusions differ from model refusals.
Point-only has no selective result. No DeltaE00 is manufactured.

Family averages are means of three per-seed metrics, not ensemble inference.
Primary paired descriptive bootstrap:2000 draws of scene IDs with replacement,
seed20260911; retain both phones and use identical draws across methods. Re-rank
within each resample for fixed80% coverage. Compare SoG versus direct C+ and
gw_ridge1 for full mean and risk80. Percentile95% intervals are exploratory,
conditional on this small field population/quality policy, not simultaneous
confidence or evidence about every phone, ISP or skin. No post-test tuning.

CPU inference is sufficient; no new latency claim from this benchmark. Existing
V2 hardware figures retain their original scope, V5 deployment performance stays
unmeasured. This is a new source-to-phone transfer screen, not a phone-trained
leave-one-camera-out experiment or reproduction of author multispectral results.
The released quantized demosaiced camera RGB is not ordinary JPEG/HEIC output.
Preserve negatives and use them to choose the next research direction.
