## Invert the current representation assumption

Existing patch voting treats the64image regions as exchangeable. It retains
global feature statistics but loses the arrangement of those regions. The new
spectral probe suggests that a shared acquisition constraint across regions
can help distinguish materials; allowing independent corrections weakens it.
This is mechanism evidence from measured spectra, not evidence of an RGB gain.

Next real-photo candidate: shared capture inference from spatial color relations,
with local shading and an absolute-color output. Compare against the strongest
plain patch model AND an ordinary spatial model with matched data/parameters/
budget. A graph or repeated solver alone is established prior art, not novelty.

Mechanism: multiple regions jointly constrain a common acquisition explanation;
local residuals preserve material differences instead of forcing all skin toward
one reference tone. Key assumption: spatial chromatic relations survive the
camera pipeline. Advantage sought: better native skinDeltaE00 under source
camera transfer without camera identity input. Likely failure: nonlinear ISP,
mixed lighting, specularities, inconsistent framing or no informative spatial
variation. Cheapest falsifier: match the model capacity and compare real-photo
source scores with intact versus scrambled spatial relations. If ordinary
spatial encoding explains all gain, reject a special-solver novelty claim.
