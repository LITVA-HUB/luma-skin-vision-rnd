Thus direct perceptual loss is not a new idea in this archive. Historical plain mixed MSE3.4771 vs DE2 3.4932; mixture mixed MSE3.4406 vs DE2 3.4843. A few transfer arms benefit; there is no universal improvement. Those are historical SOURCE TRAIN/VALIDATION roles, not new WIDE scores.
