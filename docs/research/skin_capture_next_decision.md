# Decision after54capture/metric fits

Previous goal turn and current turn are PROGRESS: new executable mechanisms,
real-reference experiments, numerical verification, a directional effect and
a newly cleared public measured-face data route change what to do next.
The unbounded innovation objective remains unachieved; no live job is implied.

## What survives and what fails

Keep the0.929M-parameter conditional mixture as a research candidate: a modest
mixed-source gain and a consistent SLR->iPod source gain exist. Do not declare
it the universal solution: it loses to plain regression in the other direction.
Additional mode labels can harm the backbone, so beating that auxiliary-task
control is insufficient. Keep the plain model as a strong comparator in every
subsequent experiment. Direct squaredDeltaE00 is not the current winner; its
reverse-camera loss is retained. No retest on the exposed400-image TEST.

## Change the representation, not only the gate

Current models infer native Lab directly from encoded image statistics. They
do not enforce that estimates correspond to plausible measured skin material
under plausible acquisition. UMINHO-HSFD's original CC BY4.0 spectra provide a
new legitimate route to test that missing assumption without proprietary data.

1. Verify original face-file/participant correspondence, band/observer/reference
   definitions and masks. Source cube is finite but includes values above1 and
   artificial zero background. Do not silently clip or call all foreground skin.
2. Inspect author artifact annotations and source-only spatial regions. Define
   skin-region validity and a small spectral-material representation before
   opening held-out face cubes. Quantify reconstruction residuals on source
   regions; distinguish diffuse-material approximation from highlights/motion.
3. Form the cheapest physical ambiguity falsifier: can different measured skin
   spectra under allowed acquisition variations yield nearly indistinguishable
   image evidence but different reference colors? Fix the allowed illuminant/
   sensor family first. This bounds what single-image inference can identify;
   it does not by itself prove a learned model or require stopping R&D.
4. If feasible, test instrument-anchored latent material plus explicit acquisition
   nuisance, with a small reconstruction/correction loop. Matched controls need
   the same spectral information, data and computation. Repeated correction
   without added physical evidence can reinforce errors; compare zero/one/
   multiple steps and an ordinary residual network.

All simulated RGB from measured spectra must be labelled DERIVED INPUT. The
core objective still requires real-photo direct skin-color accuracy; the existing
MSKCC benchmark is preserved. The new spectral route must supplement, not
replace, real camera evaluation. Ordinary unseen-phone selfies and the
median<=2/p95<=5 at80%acceptance aspiration remain unvalidated/unmet.
