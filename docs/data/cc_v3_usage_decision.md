# Separate V3 public-data usage decision

V2 used all INTEL-TAU material only for evaluation. That historical fact, its weights and controls remain unchanged. The user has now authorized further experimentation and a structurally different neural architecture. V3 may train a **separate research artifact** on previously unused INTEL-TAU images, while excluding every V1/V2 test image from all new fitting. This is an explicit revision of the earlier project usage choice, not a silent change to past provenance.

The original publisher registry directly declares **CC BY-SA4.0**: [Metax dataset record](https://metax.fairdata.fi/v3/datasets/f0570a3f-3d77-4f44-9ef1-99ab4878f17c), saved and hash-verified against milestoneea490d3. The [original license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en) permits reproduction/adaptation including commercial purposes, subject to its conditions; it is not research-only or noncommercial. Preserve attribution to Laakom, Raitoharju, Nikkanen, Iosifidis and Gabbouj, *INTEL-TAU: A Color Constancy Dataset*, IEEE Access2021, original URN and license URL. The mirror's MIT badge remains irrelevant.

This project does **not** infer that every trained model is or is not adapted licensed material. Any V3 model fitted with this data will carry explicit BY-SA source lineage and **no claim of unrestricted proprietary redistribution clearance**. No images, model, report or application will be published externally. A future distribution decision must preserve applicable attribution/ShareAlike and third-party notices. Original SimpleCube-only models keep their separate CC BY4.0 training lineage. No research-only/noncommercial weights, datasets or teachers become a production dependency.

Before decoding new pixels/GT, choose exactly the remaining128 camera-unique field images per camera from the previous256 metadata-only lists, excluding the used128. Planned new transfer4,436,408,351bytes; whole archives are unnecessary. This remains consistent with a pinned mirror; identity to the original full multipart archive checksums is unverified. The new population is not independent of every possible acquisition event merely because image IDs differ. Group/near-duplicate auditing and outer-camera separation are required before fitting.

All three outer folds will exclude their test camera from estimator fitting, architecture selection, risk fitting and calibration. Other-fold use of images follows an explicit cross-validation protocol; never treat mixed-camera random splitting as camera generalization. Source-only architecture feasibility may proceed independently using existing source train/validation rows. No new-camera results or commercial model-training success are claimed by this license decision.
