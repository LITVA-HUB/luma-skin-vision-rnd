Primary sources consulted2026-09-11:

- [Learning to Compare: Relation Network,CVPR2018](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Sung_Learning_to_Compare_CVPR_2018_paper.html):
  learning comparison metrics within episodes is established research.
- [Deep Kernel Learning,AISTATS2016](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v51/wilson16.html):
  learned representations with flexible kernels are established research.
- [Statistical ranking and combinatorial Hodge theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.1067):
  potential versus cyclic decomposition of comparisons is established mathematics.
- [The promises and pitfalls of deep kernel learning,UAI2021](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v161/ober21a.html):
  expressive kernels do not automatically guarantee robust performance.

No complete author method or published benchmark score was reproduced here.
The contribution is a controlled local skin-data falsifier, not novelty merely
from using comparisons, reference banks, graph potentials or local regression.

and comparison with standard local-regression/kernel baselines before IP claims.
