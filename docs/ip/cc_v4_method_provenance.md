# V4 mechanism and training provenance

New project implementation: scripts/cc_v4_model.py, cc_v4_geometry.py, cc_v4_experiment.py and supporting measurement/report scripts. The encoder instantiates the installed torchvision MobileNetV3-large architecture with weights=None. No external pretrained weight, foundation inference component, teacher cache, image upload or publication was used in this source screen. Local code generation and independent assistant reviews are development assistance, not evidence of human inventorship or external peer review.

The proposed mechanism uses cached local color statistics, an action-conditioned router, latent local illuminant hypotheses and exact reproduction cost;1/2/4 refinement stages reuse the image encoder. The posterior and generic action router are explicit controls. An initial log-shift representation was rejected before training because it is an exact first-layer reparameterization. The final nonlinear corrected-simplex step represents a physical inductive bias whose benefit must be measured; it adds no new observations and is approximable by a sufficiently flexible generic network.

Prior art includes candidate correction scoring (Multi-Hypothesis2020), illumination-aware augmentation (CLCC2021), scene feature modulation and learned color axes (SAFE2026), iterative residual evaluation (VLM-CC2026), color-constancy hybrid teacher distillation (TPAMI2025), derivative supervision (Sobolev2017), Self-Refine and recurrent-depth models. These ingredients and their general principles are not claimed as project inventions. [Detailed review and candidate ranking](../research/cc_v4_universal_method.md).

Training-image/GT lineage: SimpleCube++, original CC BY4.0,1126 train/119 previously used development-validation rows. No newly acquired INTEL images/GT enter V4 fitting or model selection. INTEL's original CC BY-SA4.0 and separate lineage policy remain unchanged. No research-only/NC teacher weights were adopted. Studied Apple MobileCLIP2/FastVLM and intrinsic-image assets retain their documented restrictions; study is not adoption.

All historical failures and limited V2 positives remain. A measured improvement on reused source validation alone would not establish patent novelty, industrial classification, freedom to operate, broad camera independence or physical facial colorimetry. Run snapshots, model/data hashes, selection rules, full training histories and independent numerical recomputation form an auditable research record, not a patentability opinion.
