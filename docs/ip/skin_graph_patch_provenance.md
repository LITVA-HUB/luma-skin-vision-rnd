# Graph/support and patch likelihood provenance

Both experiments reuse original MSKCC CC-BY skin images and native instrument
Lab under the existing data inventory. No new external data, pretrained weights,
copied author implementation, participant publication or paid resource is added.
Code, protocols and aggregate results are committed; photographs, identifiers,
private arrays and model checkpoints are excluded from Git.

Training-only graph regularization, patch mixing, Gaussian mixtures, Bayesian
inversion and likelihood tempering are established mechanisms. Combining them
or changing the target to skin DeltaE00 does not establish novelty. The measured
partial/negative outcome supports an auditable R&D record, not patentability.
