# Stable-color correction provenance

Original MSKCC CC-BY images/native instrument Lab and project-authored cached
features/code. Nine new small encoders are locally trained from scratch; three
existing930-step project cores are reused with exact hashes. No external
pretrained weights, private acquisition, paid resources or publication.
Identifiers, images and training/checkpoint arrays remain in ignored storage.

[Wolpert,Stacked Generalization,1992](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0893608005800231)
is primary prior art for correcting a predictor through withheld-data outputs.
Its publisher abstract was obtained in fresh search2026-09-11; an initial
abstract-page request returned403, followed by the publicly indexed publisher
article result. No access restriction was bypassed and no paper implementation
or author-reported number was imported. Earlier project OOF uncertainty and
forward-noise work is preserved and not presented as a new idea here.

The current matched experiment shows a real internal skin-color improvement,
but the OOF-superiority hypothesis loses. Standard stable-coordinate stacking,
residual learning and training on smaller-core predictions are not claimed as
inventions. A different result after method combination is empirical evidence,
not by itself technical novelty or a patentability opinion.
