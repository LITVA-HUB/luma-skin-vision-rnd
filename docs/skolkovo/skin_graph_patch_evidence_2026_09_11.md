# Graph/support and conditional patch-distribution evidence

Implemented: spatially valid training-only graph processing, observed same-site
patch support, matched plain controls, camera/mode-blind conditional patch
mixtures and sufficient-statistic controls. Runtime neural core: 924,932
parameters. These mechanisms are research hypotheses, not claimed inventions.

Measured on original public MSKCC skin photographs with actual instrument Lab:
36 neural and 48 statistical fits, with source mixed and camera-transfer
protocols. Graph/support improves its new transfer controls but loses stronger
historical models. Non-Gaussian appearance improves Gaussian controls but does
not establish a general skin-color gain. All reported errors are skin DeltaE00.
[Decision and results](../research/skin_graph_patch_next_decision.md).

Evidence: 540 exact color arrays, 48 exact statistical refits, 48,576 independent
scalar evaluation color cases, 3,096 coverage rows, spatial branch/provenance
checks, sufficient-mean checks and 348 passing tests (14 historical warnings).
The independent 400-image MSKCC test is unchanged; source reuse is explicit.

Still unvalidated: ordinary smartphone facial accuracy, cosmetics matching,
reliable calibrated refusal, general camera independence and novel contribution.
The current goal is instrument-referenced skin-color accuracy; illuminant angles
are only supporting component evidence. No legal classification is implied.
