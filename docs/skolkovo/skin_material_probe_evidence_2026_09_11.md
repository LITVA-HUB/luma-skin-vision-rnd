# Skin-material research evidence; not a product-accuracy milestone

Implemented and verified: original licensed facial spectral acquisition,
source-only manual region extraction, low-dimensional material representation
controls and shared/independent acquisition ambiguity diagnostics. These are
R&D tools supporting a possible compact image-analysis component.

Measured on public physical data:22,500spectra from three TRAIN faces,36oracle
representation controls and180derived-radiance stress fits. Full data/algorithm
hashes,40array replays,270,000independent metric cases and273passing tests are
recorded in the [report](../benchmarks/skin_spectral_probe_v1/report.md).
Original dataset attribution/license remain CC BY4.0. No new learned production
weights, inference latency or new real-photo skin accuracy are claimed.

Not validated: recovery from an ordinary RGB face photograph, unknown iPhone/
Android pipelines, population coverage, cosmetics shade matching, or product
median<=2/p95<=5DeltaE00 at80%acceptance. The established MSKCC skin test remains
unchanged; no convincing advantage of a novel method over the strongest baseline.
This evidence supports continuing research, not approved legal classification,
patentability, product readiness, or proof of technical novelty.
