# Measured-material image experiment evidence

Implemented: compact four-hypothesis image models with ordinary, tangent,
nonlinear measured-skin spectrum, and residual decoders. All use one image.
Measured: 45 exploratory real MSKCC instrument-Lab fits across three seeds and
three source camera protocols. The nonlinear material method does not beat the
strongest controls consistently; no innovative performance claim is supported.
[Full results](../benchmarks/skin_material_image_v1/report.md).

A known-target TRAIN solver can reconstruct all 248 site colors within 0.01
DeltaE00, but uses the supplied answer. It is a representation diagnostic,
not image accuracy or product evidence. The independent skin test is unchanged.

Unvalidated: ordinary phone facial accuracy, universality, reliable per-image
error bounds, and a superior novel mechanism. Compact normalization/reliability
remains candidate technology positioning, not an approved legal classification.
