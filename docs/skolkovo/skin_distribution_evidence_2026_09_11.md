# Real skin conditional-color experiment evidence

Implemented: one-image compact ordinary, single-Gaussian and four-component
native-Lab estimators, plus target-free approximate expected-DeltaE decisions.
Measured:36fits on real MSKCC images with instrument skin references. Distribution
models do not provide a universal accuracy gain; one Gaussian has a partial
mixed-source uncertainty-ranking benefit which fails under transfer.
[Full evidence](../benchmarks/skin_distribution_v1/report.md).

The independent400image/10person result is unchanged. The source screen is
heavily reused and exploratory. Actual ordinary-phone facial accuracy, reliable
per-image error bounds, novel superiority and product target remain unvalidated.
The technology positioning is still a candidate, not an approved legal category.

935,453stored parameters, no foundation model/camera metadata input. Training
fits locally; inference/export performance not claimed for this branch.
