# Additional evidence: actual skin-color representation controls

Implemented: compact native-Lab prediction, training-only perturbation controls,
rank-dependence context with an absolute-color path, and source-only camera
transfer evaluation. Two families add 72 fits with genuine MSKCC instrument
references. These are color-error experiments, not illuminant-angle claims.

Measured: both families are negative as universal improvements over strong
ordinary controls. One-direction gains do not demonstrate camera independence.
All 216 color and 216 risk prediction arrays replay exactly; 12,672 scalar
CIEDE2000 cases and 432 fixed-coverage cases were independently checked.
The nuisance test precision amendment is retained with original evidence.

Independent evidence remains the earlier 400-image / 10-new-person MSKCC test:
primary mean 4.4570 DeltaE00; 80% mean 4.1591. Strong ordinary fusion reaches
4.3005 / 4.1447, so there is no proposed-method superiority claim. New source
fits must not be presented as fresh independent confirmation.

Still unvalidated: ordinary facial photographs across unseen iPhone/Android
devices, calibrated per-image reliability, and the proposed product accuracy
target. Clinical capture geometry is materially different from ordinary selfies.
No legal classification or patent novelty is asserted by these experiments.
