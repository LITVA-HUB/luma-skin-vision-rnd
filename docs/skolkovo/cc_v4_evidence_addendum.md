# V4 compact correction and reliability research evidence

Candidate positioning: compact adaptive color normalization and reliability estimation for analysis of color-sensitive visual objects, with facial skin analysis and cosmetics recommendation as an intended first commercial application. This wording is not an approved legal classification.

**Implemented:** a 3,097,189-parameter image encoder with cached local evidence, nonlinear correction-conditioned routing, latent illuminant hypotheses and 1/2/4-stage refinement. Matched posterior and generic-action controls, reproduction-cost supervision, refusal of invalid inputs, immutable experiment receipts and independent verification are implemented. A modern-training/prior-art review distinguishes established ingredients from the proposed mechanism.

**Measured on real public data:** SimpleCube++ under original CC BY 4.0, with 1,126 training and 119 reused development-validation images. Three 120-epoch runs gave proposed step-2 mean reproduction error 2.3889°, versus posterior 2.1809° and generic feedback 2.3169°. Proposed raw risk80 was 2.000°, versus posterior 1.895°. The proposed mechanism has not shown an advantage. More refinement steps do not consistently reduce real error despite reducing predicted risk. This is negative engineering research evidence, not independent confirmation of a superior technology.

**Measured engineering feasibility:** RTX 4060 training peak 765.46 MiB including 233.45 MiB of cached source data; 12,599,089-byte checkpoint; proposed step-2 device-resident 128×128 RGB batch-1 median 6.104–6.203 ms across three sessions of 100 measurements; peak PyTorch inference allocation 29.12 MiB including model and four input thumbnails. Driver/context memory, decode, disk, upload and correction rendering are excluded. Other mode/stage timings show desktop variability. No V4 ONNX, TensorRT or quantization result is claimed. The repository passes 185 tests; independently computed angles and CPU checkpoint replay agree within recorded numerical tolerances.

**Still to validate:** multi-seed paired architecture effects, selected-action calibration against strong C+, independently locked scene/camera/processing transfer, robustness to unknown smartphone ISP, measured facial skin Lab/DeltaE and cosmetics utility. No facial dataset was used. Public illuminant GT does not prove skin-color accuracy.

**IP and next decision:** candidate scoring, feature modulation, recurrence, uncertainty, equivariance, teacher transfer and derivative supervision have prior art. Novelty, patentability and freedom to operate are not established. The initial linear-transport draft was rejected as an exact MLP reparameterization before training. The implemented nonlinear mechanism failed this screen; the next investigation focuses on policy-selected mistakes and stronger scene context with matched controls. Existing bounded positive V2 camera-transfer evidence and V3/synthetic negatives remain unchanged.
