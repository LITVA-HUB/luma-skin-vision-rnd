# Evidence: relative skin matching versus absolute skin color

IMPLEMENTED: reproducible matched-pair controls, comparative feature analysis,
48 leave-one-person-out linear fits and additive/cycle degeneracy checks.

MEASURED ON PUBLIC REAL DATA: original MSKCC TRAIN24people/966images,1,421
same-site capture pairs and18,229 between-site pairs. Ridge36 achieves70.08%
paired preference but actual mean5.4259/p9511.4364 DeltaE00 in exploratory
person-excluded cross-validation. Pair recognition is not percent skin-color
accuracy. No same-site cross-camera pairs exist; original clinical acquisition
does not establish ordinary smartphone facial accuracy.

UNCHANGED INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE: primary4.4570/80%4.1591 versus stronger ordinary
fusion4.3005/4.1447 DeltaE00. No new independent model or novelty advantage.

STILL TO VALIDATE: compact learned improvements over matched C+, reliable
refusal, cross-camera facial skin measurement on ordinary phones, and cosmetic
shade decisions. Reference-bank/relational ideas remain unverified hypotheses.
