# ISSA measured-material evidence, 2026-09-11

Candidate positioning remains compact adaptive color normalization and
reliability estimation for color-sensitive objects, with facial skin analysis
and cosmetics recommendation as the first intended application. This wording
is not an approved legal classification or a patentability conclusion.

## Implemented and measured

Original publicly released CC BY4.0 ISSA v4 acquired, attributed and hashed.
Read-only metadata loader, frozen subject-code/source roles, spectral-support
audit and native colorimetric formula replay implemented. No proprietary data.
8,680 TRAIN and1,822 VALIDATION spectral records decoded; TEST/reserved source
records untouched. The original workbook is unchanged and uncommitted.

Fifteen fixed spectral-compression configurations plus a mean-spectrum control
were evaluated. On1,156 actual common-support validation records, three linear
components preserve supplied skin Lab with mean0.4495/p951.0564 DeltaE00. Eight
optical-density components yield mean0.02365/p950.06113. These are locally
measured full-spectrum oracle controls, not photo color recovery. The original
reference uses the workbook-specific common-band white, not MSKCC's observer.

Independent scalar checks cover18,496 model color errors. Numerical consistency
does not erase dataset identity problems:2,107 local codes versus2,113 reported
people, demographic coding conflicts and17 cross-split exact spectral matches.
A post-hoc exclusion of two linked validation codes removes17records outside
the color subset, leaving all color scores unchanged. Both analyses are saved.

## What remains unproved

No accuracy gain for an RGB photograph is established by this milestone.
No ordinary iPhone/Android facial-color validation, universal camera claim,
clinical claim, unique physical theory or protected invention is established.
Skin-specific optical manifolds and spectral compression have substantial
prior art. A future method must beat matched ordinary image baselines on real
instrument labels and calibrated rejection, with a fresh independent protocol.

The preserved independent MSKCC result remains primary mean4.4570 and80%
selective mean4.1591 DeltaE00. Ordinary fusion remains stronger4.3005/4.1447.
The negative synthetic and teacher results remain archived. This milestone
adds verified material-representation evidence and rejects the tested two-
dimensional PCA bases for precise color preservation. It does not refute all
nonlinear two-variable optical models or improve the reported photo result.
