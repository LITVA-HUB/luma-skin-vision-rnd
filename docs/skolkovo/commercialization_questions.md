# Commercialization hypotheses — 2026-09-10

No customer demand, revenue, market size or benefit is asserted. The official Foundation review considers commercialization potential; evidence must come from actual customers and a viable delivery model. [Official process](https://sk.ru/applicants-actions/).

| Hypothesis / potential buyer | Evidence to collect | Failure or pivot signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Luma users benefit from fewer unreliable color measurements | Current flow completion/retake rates, user interviews, error-linked recommendation outcomes | Measurement errors are not a meaningful source of product failure |
| Retailers or brands pay for a shade-selection API/SDK | Named buyer interviews, pilot scope, budgets, integration constraints, willingness to pay | Existing controlled capture/classical calibration is sufficient or no budget owner exists |
| Consultation stations tolerate controlled capture | Pilot with calibrated device/light and operator workflow | Capture friction exceeds benefit or staffing economics fail |
| Device-adaptive service reduces partner calibration burden | Compare engineering/support cost and failure rates before/after on partner devices | Per-device calibration remains mandatory and cannot be supported economically |

Founder inputs needed: target customer/geography, price/budget assumptions, existing anonymized funnel metrics, named partner evidence, acquisition/distribution plan, data/equipment budget, team commitments and legal entity facts. Do not fabricate them from product context.

Validate stages separately: skin measurement → trustworthy product swatch/coverage/finish/oxidation data → recommendation decision → user outcome. A low ΔE00 alone cannot establish shade-match accuracy, conversion uplift or return reduction. Track useful coverage and recapture burden alongside accuracy; include privacy, on-device/server costs, maintenance for new camera ISPs and model drift.

Potential deliverables: local engine, server API, mobile SDK, or controlled capture station. Choose after latency, privacy and customer constraints are measured. Market sizing and regulated/medical positioning are not part of this first non-medical experiment.
