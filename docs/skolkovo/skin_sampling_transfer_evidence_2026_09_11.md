# Source acquisition-transfer evidence for actual skin color

Implemented: combined person/color training allocation and five matched sampling
controls; fixed-budget known/unseen acquisition protocols; no camera input at
inference. Actual instrument-native Lab and DeltaE00 are the endpoint.

Measured:54 source fits and90 evaluation arrays. The combination has mixed mean
3.7435 vs strongest matched control3.7864, but loses on SLR-to-iPod6.1545 vs5.4877
and reverse6.4074 vs6.0825. Selective reverse80% error6.7609 is worse than full
coverage. This is a documented negative universal-transfer result, not a claim
of general skin-color precision. [Decision](../research/skin_sampling_transfer_next_decision.md).

Audited:90 exact arrays, six complete state refits,14256 scalar color cases,
476490 TRAIN density pairs,540 coverage rows and144 sampling-mass identities.
359 tests pass. Core929,297 parameters; measured feature-training peak106.23MiB.
No new isolated inference benchmark or export.

Still unvalidated: ordinary facial smartphones, robust unknown acquisition,
trustworthy refusal, cosmetics recommendations and technical novelty advantage.
The independent test is unchanged. No approved legal classification is implied.
