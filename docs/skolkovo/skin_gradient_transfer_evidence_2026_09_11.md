# TRAIN-only optimization-mechanism evidence

IMPLEMENTED: reproducible group-gradient and finite-update diagnostics on the
compact direct skin-color estimator, with size-preserving random-group controls,
exact model-state restoration and independent scalar color verification.

MEASURED ON PUBLIC REAL DATA: six frozen models,24 TRAIN partitions,48 objective
cases and288 transient interventions using genuine MSKCC native instrument Lab.
116 of288 steps improve their own training objective while increasing pooled
TRAIN mean skin DeltaE00. Group conflict is not consistently person-specific
under single-camera controls. This constrains R&D hypotheses; it is not improved
held-out accuracy or evidence of a novel deployable mechanism.

UNCHANGED INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE: primary mean4.4570/80%4.1591 versus ordinary
fusion4.3005/4.1447 DeltaE00. Novel method superiority remains unproved.

STILL UNVALIDATED: ordinary facial skin measurement on unseen phones, dependable
cosmetic shade decisions and calibration-free camera independence. Angular
illumination results cannot substitute for instrument-referenced skin accuracy.

The broader photometric/color-reliability technology positioning remains a
candidate technical description, not an approved legal classification. Original
MSKCC CC-BY provenance is retained; no new outside code/weights or data used.
Gradient matching and gradient-variance matching are known prior art.
