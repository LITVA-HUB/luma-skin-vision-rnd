# Conditional appearance inversion: technical evidence

Implemented: compact statistical forward appearance models, camera/mode-blind
inverse inference, person-excluded forward-noise estimation, genuine native-Lab
palette decisions and direct regression controls with identical empirical
support. These are established Bayesian mechanisms, not asserted inventions.

Measured: 108 source fits on original MSKCC skin photographs with actual
instrument-native Lab, plus 36 direct models under two support controls. Mixed
mean 3.8663 versus direct 3.9046 and hull-constrained direct 3.8947 is a small,
inconclusive source gain. Stronger compact image models remain better. Neither
transfer nor selective results establish universal camera-independent precision.
[Full report](../benchmarks/skin_appearance_inverse_v1/report.md).

Still unvalidated: ordinary facial selfies, unseen modern smartphones, cosmetics
matching, trustworthy calibrated acceptance, novelty and deployment readiness.
Illuminant angular accuracy remains separate from this actual skin-color endpoint.
The independent MSKCC test is unchanged; no fresh confirmation is claimed.

Reproducibility evidence includes exact refits, scalar color checks, Gaussian
density checks, convex-projection KKT checks and 343 passing tests. It supports
implementation integrity and a documented negative/partial experiment, not
patentability or an approved Skolkovo legal classification. Original MSKCC CC-BY;
no new external data/weights, participant publication or paid cloud resources.
