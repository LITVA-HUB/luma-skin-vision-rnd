# Actual skin-color risk crossing and loss-field evidence

Implemented: compact native-Lab image models, Gaussian color-risk crossing with
matched ordinary pairs, and directly supervised candidate-color loss fields.
Real instrument references are preserved; candidate losses are mathematical
derivations of those references, not additional measured examples.

Measured:90fixed source crossing endpoints and36matched field fits. A mixed
source80%coverage difference3.1954 versus3.3625 is inconclusive by descriptive
patient resampling and does not persist across camera transfer. Loss fields
do not show universal superiority. [Research decision](../research/skin_risk_field_next_decision.md).

Verified: full327-test suite, exact prediction replays, independent scalar
CIEDE2000, quadrature, Gram and grid checks. These establish reproducibility of
the experiment, not innovation or commercial color accuracy.

Not validated: ordinary phone facial color accuracy, universal camera invariance,
calibrated per-image error guarantees, superior novel mechanism, and product
precision targets. Independent MSKCC test remains unchanged and must not be
retuned. The broader color-normalization/reliability positioning remains a
candidate description, not an approved legal classification.

Original MSKCC CC-BY; no new external datasets or pretrained weights. No external
publishing or paid compute. Internal experimental evidence and negative findings
are preserved separately from commercial claims.
